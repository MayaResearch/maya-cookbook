# Community conduct

Be respectful, constructive and welcoming. Critique code and evidence, not people. Do not harass, discriminate, threaten, publish personal information or share private customer content.

Report conduct concerns privately to [charan@mayaresearch.ai](mailto:charan@mayaresearch.ai). Maintainers may remove harmful material and restrict participation. Before public launch Maya should assign the responsible maintainers and confirm the reporting process.
